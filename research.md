My work sits between gravitational-wave astrophysics and the practical problem of extracting faint signals from noisy instruments. Most of it is in preparation for ESA's [LISA](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/lisa) mission, building on my experience with its technology demonstrator, [LISA Pathfinder](https://sci.esa.int/web/lisa-pathfinder).


### Gravitational-wave astronomy

Studying the properties of our Galaxy through the gravitational waves emitted by compact stellar objects, such as binary neutron stars, white dwarfs, and neutron star–black hole binaries of a few solar masses. Analysis of compact binary populations (white-dwarf and black-hole binaries) via hierarchical statistical methods. Cosmological gravitational-wave signals.

### Data analysis & instrumentation

Signal processing and matched filtering; stochastic algorithms based on Markov chain Monte Carlo and machine-learning techniques; GPU-accelerated computational methods. Search and parameter estimation of stochastic gravitational-wave signals in detector noise. Calibration of gravitational-wave detectors, modelling of noise sources, and construction of noise budgets. Data analysis methods for multi-messenger astronomy.
